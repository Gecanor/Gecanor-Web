How to create your own cosmetics brand in Spain

By David Castillo, CEO, Gecanor Beauty Tech · Published on 24 November 2026

Creating your own cosmetics brand in Spain comes down to seven decisions: which product and for whom, white label or a custom formula, which laboratory manufactures it, how you comply with EU regulation, which packaging you use, how many units you produce and how you launch. With a partner coordinating everything, you can start from 500 units per product.

At Gecanor we work with brands starting from scratch and with beauty clinics and salons that want their own line. These are the questions we are asked almost every time, in the order it makes sense to answer them.

1. Define the product, the audience and the retail price

Before talking to a laboratory, write a one-page brief: product type (serum, cream, cleanser, mask…), who it is for, what it promises, where it will be sold (online, in-salon, pharmacy, distributor) and at what price. The retail price sets the maximum unit cost you can afford, and that cost drives the formula, the packaging and the volume.

2. Choose between white label and a custom formula

White label (the lab's formula) Custom formula
What it is An existing, tested formula that you put your brand on A formula created for your brand from your brief
Lead time Shorter: the formula already has its studies Longer: development, samples and revisions
Differentiation Limited: other brands may sell the same product High: an exclusive product
Formula ownership The laboratory's Can be yours if agreed by contract
Best for Testing the market, salon lines, first launches Brands with their own concept and plans to grow

Many brands combine both: they start with white-label products and develop their hero product as a custom formula.

3. Choose the manufacturing laboratory

Don't choose on the lowest minimum order alone. Check that the laboratory follows good manufacturing practice (ISO 22716) and has filed the statement of activities with the Spanish medicines agency, AEMPS, which is mandatory in Spain for anyone who manufactures, fills or labels cosmetics or imports them from outside the EU. Also ask who will own the formula, what the unit price includes and which documents you receive with each batch.

4. Comply with EU regulation before you sell

Every cosmetic sold in the EU is governed by Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009. In practice, before the product goes on sale you need:

  • A responsible person in the EU, accountable for the product's compliance. If you sell under your own brand, this is usually your company.
  • A safety assessment signed by a qualified professional.
  • The Product Information File (PIF), which you must keep for 10 years after the last batch is placed on the market.
  • The notification on the EU CPNP portal.

We explain it step by step in Legal requirements to sell cosmetics in the EU.

5. Design the packaging and the label

Packaging is not just aesthetics: it must be compatible with the formula (checked with a compatibility test) and leave room for the mandatory information, such as the ingredient list, batch number, responsible person and the date of minimum durability or the period after opening (PAO). Watch the minimums: custom packaging or a custom colour often requires more units than the manufacturing itself.

6. Work out the minimum order and the unit cost

The unit price of a contract-manufactured cosmetic usually includes raw materials, manufacturing, filling, labelling, batch coding and the packaging. On top of that come one-off costs: formula development, regulatory studies, safety assessment, design and photography. At Gecanor the minimum order starts at 500 units per product. The final cost depends mainly on the product type, the complexity of the formula, the packaging and the volume: always ask for a quote broken down into unit and one-off costs.

7. Plan the timeline and prepare the launch

The timeline depends mainly on whether you start from a white-label formula, whose studies are already done, or a custom development, which adds samples, adjustments and stability testing. While it is being manufactured, prepare the website, photos, copy (without claims you cannot substantiate) and your sales channel.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order to manufacture cosmetics under my own brand?

It depends on the laboratory and the packaging. At Gecanor it starts at 500 units per product, so you can launch without large volumes.

Do I need to be a laboratory or have facilities to own a cosmetics brand?

No. You can outsource manufacturing to an authorised laboratory. What you do need is a responsible person who ensures compliance with Regulation 1223/2009.

Will I own the formula?

With white label, no: it belongs to the laboratory. With a custom formula it can be yours if agreed; at Gecanor the intellectual property of custom formulas belongs to your brand.

Do I have to register the cosmetic with the health authorities?

There is no prior authorisation per product: it is notified on the EU CPNP portal before it is sold, and the PIF is kept available to the authorities.

Can I sell across the EU with the same notification?

Yes. The CPNP notification covers the whole European Union, although the label must be in the language of each country where you sell.
