Regulatory & compliance services

We ensure that your products comply with all the rigorous requirements of the European Union's cosmetic regulations (EC) No 1223/2009. Our comprehensive regulatory package covers all necessary testing, documentation, and registration to legally place your product on the market.

01 Stability & Compatibility Studies
• Product Stability Test: To ensure the product maintains its physical, chemical, and microbiological quality under various conditions over time.• Container Compatibility Test: To verify the compatibility between the product and its final packaging, ensuring no adverse reactions occur.
02 Microbiological Testing
Performed on each production batch to guarantee the product is free from harmful microorganisms and meets the required quality standards.
03 Challenge Test (Preservative Efficacy Test)
This test evaluates the effectiveness of the product’s preservative system against microbial contamination during its shelf-life and consumer use.
04 Patch Test
A skin compatibility test performed on human volunteers to ensure the product does not cause irritation or allergic reactions.
05 Product Information File (PIF)
Compilation of the complete technical dossier for the cosmetic product, containing all data, reports, and information required by the EU regulation.
06 Safety Assessment & CPNP Registration
• Safety Evaluation: A thorough safety assessment of the final product, conducted and signed by a qualified toxicologist or pharmacist.• CPNP Registration: Notification of the product in the European Cosmetic Products Notification Portal (CPNP), which is mandatory before placing it on the market.
